How to add a ticket

Say or type the task, then any of: a priority (urgent, high, medium, low), an area (home, money, health, family, career, growth, fun), a time (30 min, 2 hours), or "blocked: reason". "Remind me to…" on any Google device lands here too. Ticking a ticket marks it done; nothing is deleted.
