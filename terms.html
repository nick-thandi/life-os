Life OS terms

Life OS is a private family organiser run by one household for its own members. It is not a public service and is not for sale.

Who can use it

Only people the household has invited can sign in. Each person signs in with their own Google account and can stop at any time by removing access in their Google Account.

What it does

It shows the family's day from Google Calendar, keeps each person's to-do list in their own Google Tasks, and suggests tasks from new email where the person has allowed that. How it handles data is set out on the privacy page.

No guarantees

Life OS is a home project. It may be unavailable or get things wrong. Check anything important, such as a bill or a deadline, at its source before acting on it.
