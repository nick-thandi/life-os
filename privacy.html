Life OS privacy

Life OS is a private family organiser used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

When a family member signs in with Google, Life OS reads that person's Google Calendar and Google Tasks so it can show the day and the in-tray. If the person also allows it, Life OS reads the sender, subject, date and one-line preview of new Gmail messages to spot things that need doing. It does not read message bodies or attachments.

What it changes

Life OS adds and updates tasks in the person's own Google Tasks, and adds calendar events when the person approves them. It never sends, moves, labels or deletes email.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted in the family's own database. A short record of each action (for example, the title of a ticket it filed) is kept there so it can be undone. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Anyone can remove Life OS's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services".
